  1. =download.py= takes =--kind= with one or more of =video audio subtitle= in place of =-q audio= and =-s=
  2. =download.py= takes =--quality best|1080p|720p|480p= for video
  3. =download.py= takes =--lang= for the subtitle language
  4. =download.py= with no URL argument asks yes or no for video and audio and subtitles then asks each option
  5. =CMD.org= has one download block that runs the prompts

* DESIGN
  - =CMD.org= =Download with options= runs =download.py= =main()= with no URL
  - =main()= calls =parse_arguments()= then =prompt_options()= when =args.url= is =None=
  - =prompt_options()= calls =prompt_yes_no()= for video and audio and subtitles
  - =prompt_options()= calls =prompt_choice()= for quality and browser
  - =main()= calls =download()= once per entry of =args.kinds=
  - =main()= skips the audio =download()= when video and audio are both chosen
  - =main()= calls =extract_audio()= with the mp4 path that =download()= returns
  - =KINDS= and =QUALITIES= and =BROWSERS= are read by =parse_arguments()= and =prompt_options()=
